# Model card: FarmConnect synthetic ML demonstrations

## Status and intended use
These are real fitted scikit-learn regression estimators trained on generated synthetic classroom records. They demonstrate reproducible training, artifact loading, model comparison, and API inference only. They are **not validated agricultural market models** and must not be used for pricing, purchasing, production, or financial decisions.

## Models
- Price demo: predicts a synthetic illustrative INR/kg value from product ID, canonical category ID, month, quantity (kg), and quality grade.
- Demand demo: predicts synthetic next-month quantity (kg) from product ID, category ID, month, and a synthetic recent monthly quantity.
- Training compares a mean baseline, Random Forest, and HistGradientBoosting using a fixed seed and a held-out final 20% of generated rows. Since generated rows are not observations from a market, reported metrics are only code-pipeline diagnostics.

## Data and limitations
All records in `data/synthetic/` are generated by `training/train_models.py`. No real market-price dataset, real order history, location-specific supply, or official source is included. No claims of market accuracy, seasonality validity, fairness, or external generalization are made. Price inputs do not include geographic location or a real market feed. Outputs are returned only under `/demo/*` and include `synthetic_demo_only` labels.

## Retraining
From `ml-service`, install `requirements.txt`, then run `python training/train_models.py`. Model artifacts are stored under `models/`. Run `pytest -q` to check API validation and service health.


## Product-specific synthetic revision (2.0.0)

The current training script generates 6,000 synthetic rows per model using a fixed seed and a stable 30-product demonstration catalogue. Product ID is included as a model feature, in addition to category and month; price also uses quantity and quality grade, while demand uses recent monthly quantity. The included CSV files and `.joblib` artifacts are generated classroom assets, not collected market or order data. Evaluation is on the last 20% of generated rows and measures fit to the synthetic generation process only.
